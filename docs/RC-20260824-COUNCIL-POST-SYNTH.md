# RC-20260824-COUNCIL-POST-SYNTH

Council is held by default. Normal Room sends force `councilMode=off`. The green control beside AI Warehouse is a one-shot action: after independent AI answers are visible, clicking `Council 보류` re-submits the latest user question once with `councilMode=on` for a Council final synthesis, then returns the control to hold state. At least two selected AIs are required.
