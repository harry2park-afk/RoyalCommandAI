# RESEARCH BASIS — INTERNAL, NOT CUSTOMER CONTRACT LANGUAGE

This draft was structured after reviewing current Australian regulatory materials and common SaaS / AI contract architecture. It does not copy another provider's terms.

Primary Australian legal/regulatory sources reviewed for this draft include:

- Australian Competition and Consumer Commission — **Contracts / Unfair contract terms** (standard form consumer and small business contracts; prohibition and penalties applying to unfair terms).
- Australian Competition and Consumer Commission — **Consumer rights and guarantees** (consumer guarantees cannot be removed by blanket “no refunds” wording).
- Australian Competition and Consumer Commission — **Warranties** (consumer guarantees and mandatory wording requirements where applicable).
- Office of the Australian Information Commissioner — **Australian Privacy Principles Guidelines**, including APP 1 and APP 8.
- **Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth), Schedule 2 — Australian Consumer Law**, including unfair contract term provisions.
- **Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth)**, including electronic-signature principles.
- **Electronic Transactions Act 2000 (NSW)**, including electronic-signature principles.

## Drafting principles applied

- recurring subscription fees are separated from Human Professional fees;
- first paid month is charged in advance at activation;
- the Customer may test a Room before activation, subject to reasonable test limits;
- higher-effort Custom / Professional Rooms may use a clearly disclosed Minimum Term;
- change-of-mind refunds are distinguished from non-excludable Australian Consumer Law remedies;
- blanket “no refund under any circumstances” wording is intentionally avoided;
- unilateral variation and liability provisions are intentionally restrained pending Australian legal review;
- customer-facing AI is required to disclose paid options and obtain approval rather than silently adding charges;
- cross-border AI/cloud processing is recognised as a privacy issue requiring APP 8 analysis rather than a generic waiver;
- acceptance is versioned and recorded electronically; and
- this draft is not yet connected to a production “I agree” screen.
